# Art Direction

## Intent
Minimalist, tactile, cozy, quiet. Avoid casino/mobile-ad visual language, excessive gradients, reward explosions, or high-saturation urgency.

## Reference
The old prototype demonstrates useful information density and board composition but is not a final style target. The desired mood is closer to contemplative puzzle/toy experiences such as the user's original Zen Bound reference: restrained motion, soft surfaces, and deliberate spacing.

## Accessibility
- Symbol shape is primary; color is secondary.
- Provide sufficient luminance contrast.
- Cat and arrow states must remain distinguishable in common color-vision deficiencies.
- Avoid ultra-thin icons at small board sizes.

## Asset policy
Keep gameplay logic independent from final art so placeholder vector/icons can be replaced without rule changes.
