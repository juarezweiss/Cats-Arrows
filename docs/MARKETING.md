# Marketing Strategy

## Positioning hypothesis
"A calm deduction puzzle where cats hide behind numbers and arrows — every board can be solved by logic, never guessing."

## Before paid acquisition
- prove Day-1/Day-7 retention baseline;
- confirm tutorial completion and first-puzzle conversion;
- build App Store/Play Store creative assets;
- instrument attribution/analytics with privacy review;
- collect organic qualitative feedback.

## Initial creative angles
- satisfying row/column elimination;
- one-arrow deduction transformations;
- "Can you find every cat without guessing?";
- cozy tactile theme/ASMR-like interaction;
- short before/after board solves.

## Experiment discipline
Each campaign/test needs: audience hypothesis, creative hypothesis, success metric, spend cap, stop condition, and post-test decision. Do not scale based only on cheap installs; optimize against downstream engagement/retention when sample size permits.
